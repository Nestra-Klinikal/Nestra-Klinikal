Who We Are

Nestra Klinikal (NK)

Nestra Klinikal (NK) is a Clinical Research and Quality Management System Organization (CRO/QMSO) incorporated in the Federal Republic of Nigeria in 2012, but started operations in November 2018.

Driven by our extensive experience, international training, exposure and passion for developing and implementing different quality systems, we aspire to help organizations, institutions, clinical research sites, medical diagnostic laboratories, pharmaceutical companies and associated agencies develop and achieve desired quality management system and quality assurance goals.

We do this by performing gap analysis, training, developing organizational-focused programs to close identified gaps, auditing, mentoring, monitoring, assessing and evaluating different QMS applicable to specific organizations — including GCP Quality Assurance, GMP, Validation/Verification, GCLP, GLP, ISO 15189 and ISO 17025 quality systems.

Our Approach

How we build capability

We treat quality management as a discipline built in stages. Learners and partner organizations move through a structured pathway — from foundational certificate training through to advanced diploma-level mastery — while specialist tracks such as Phlebotomy are delivered as their own certification, including mandatory supervised practical hours before certification is awarded.

We work directly with organizations as well as through institutional partners, always retaining ownership of our curriculum, training materials and academic records to protect the integrity and consistency of every certificate we issue.
