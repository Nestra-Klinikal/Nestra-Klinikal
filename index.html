About Nestra Klinikal (NK)

Building quality management capability across Nigeria's health & research sector

Nestra Klinikal (NK) is a Clinical Research and Quality Management System Organization (CRO/QMSO) incorporated in the Federal Republic of Nigeria in 2012, and operational since November 2018.

Driven by extensive experience, international training and exposure, we help organizations, institutions, clinical research sites, medical diagnostic laboratories, pharmaceutical companies and associated agencies develop and achieve their quality management system and quality assurance goals — through gap analysis, training, auditing, mentoring, monitoring and evaluation.
