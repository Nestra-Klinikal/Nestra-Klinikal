How It Works

A structured, protected partnership

Every institutional partnership runs on a formal Collaboration, Training Services and Revenue Share Agreement — not an informal MoU. This defines fee handling, reporting and audit rights clearly, so both sides know exactly where they stand.

Nestra Klinikal retains ownership of the curriculum, training materials, academic records and program IP, while the partner institution manages its own student relationships and campus delivery. This keeps certification standards consistent across every partner site.
